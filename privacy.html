Privacy policy

Mail Bridge · Last updated 18 November 2026

Who uses it

This tool is for its owner's personal use only. It only works with Gmail accounts that the owner signs in himself.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and searches emails, saves drafts, sends emails the owner approves, and creates Gmail filters the owner approves. It cannot delete emails.

What it stores

It does not store, log or sell email content. Sign-in keys are kept in the owner's computer keychain. A list of connected account names and sign-in dates is kept on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Email data is not sold or shared with anyone. When the owner asks his AI assistant about an email, that email's content is sent to the assistant to answer his request.

Google data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
